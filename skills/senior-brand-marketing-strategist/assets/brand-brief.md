# Brand strategy brief — reusable template

**Brand / product:**  
**Scope / country / languages:**

**Approval state:** OBSERVED / HYPOTHESIS / PROPOSED / APPROVED.

## 1. Business problem and measurable objective
What user/business decision should become easier? Baseline known? If not, label unknown.

## 2. Evidence ledger
| ID | Assertion | Source/date | Status and strength | May publish? | Conflict / action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | | | | | |

## 3. Audiences and actual alternatives
| Role / segment | Situation / JTBD | Risk / selection criteria | Real alternatives | Proof needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Positioning options
| Hypothesis | Frame/category | Target benefit | Distinctive mechanism | RTB source or GAP | Strategic risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Chosen direction (if approved)
One sentence; three message pillars maximum; what we do NOT claim.

## 6. Brand personality, verbal examples and visual consequences
Name attributes with Do/Don't, use cases, reference constraints, sketch/typography/logo considerations.

## 7. Critical review
Contradictory audience/claim? Competitor could say same? IP/privacy risk? Change cost? What evidence would disprove direction?
