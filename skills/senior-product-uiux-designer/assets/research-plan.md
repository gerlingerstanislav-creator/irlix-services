# UX Research Plan — [decision]

**Decision to inform:** ...  **What will change based on findings:** ...

## Known vs unknown
Evidence observed; hypotheses; excluded assumptions and source quality.

## Participants
User segments and why, recruitment criteria, accessibility and platform coverage, ethics/privacy/consent, sample rationale (do not blindly demand N=5).

## Method
Contextual interviews / observation / task test / tree test / usability benchmark / analytics; reasons and limits.

## Tasks and prompts
Realistic, not instructing participants which UI controls to use; no confidential fixture data. Define expected outcome and failure modes, not an answer key shown to participants.

## Metrics and capture
Task success criteria, serious errors, assistance, time, observed confusion, quotes anonymized, conditions. Quantitative inference only with justified design.

## Synthesis and action
Themes, supporting evidence, severity/confidence, disconfirming evidence, potential changes, next experiments, owner and action due date.
